Tools of the Trade

Small, optional helpers for AdequateFootwear's geocaches. They take some of the tedium out of a puzzle. The puzzle itself is still yours to solve.

You never need these. Everything required to solve each cache is on its cache page and in the field. Each tool runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and it keeps working once it has loaded, even if you lose signal.

  • Read the Sign

    For (line, word, character) sign puzzles: type the sign, paste the formula, and see what the sign says, with a trace of every triple.
