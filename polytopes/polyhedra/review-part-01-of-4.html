      Where the facts come from

      Each uniform polyhedron here was built from its Wythoff symbol, the classic kaleidoscope construction, and its counts, sizes and symmetry were worked out from the result. Every count, radius and angle was then checked against Wolfram's polyhedron data. The Catalan solids are the exact duals of the Archimedean ones. Prisms and antiprisms are built in your browser as you ask for them.
